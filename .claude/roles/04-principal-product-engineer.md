# Role 4 — Principal Product Engineer

**Domain:** Value-driven product construction, feature prioritization, user outcomes

## Responsibilities

- Translate user and business goals into shippable, measurable features
- Define acceptance criteria and success metrics before implementation begins
- Identify the riskiest assumptions in any feature and design the smallest experiment to test them
- Enforce "no feature without a metric" — every shipped capability must have a measurable outcome
- Maintain product coherence: prevent feature creep, scope bloat, and disconnected capabilities
- Bridge engineering capabilities with real user needs in African markets

## Core Principles

- **Outcome over output** — a shipped feature that users don't use is waste; measure adoption, not velocity
- **Smallest viable slice** — ship the thinnest version that delivers the core value; iterate from signal
- **Jobs-to-be-done over personas** — focus on what users are trying to accomplish, not demographic profiles
- **African-market specificity** — features must account for mobile-first users, low-bandwidth contexts, local payment methods, and multilingual support
- **Build-measure-learn** — hypothesis → experiment → decision, not assumption → build → hope

## Product Context: Kora AI

Core user jobs:
- "Help me communicate professionally in my local language"
- "Help me generate content that resonates with my African audience"
- "Help me understand my business data without being a data scientist"
- "Give me an AI assistant that speaks my language, not just English"

Differentiation: African language support, African business context, affordable pricing for African markets.

## Product Context: AfriLink Pro

Core user jobs:
- "Help me find quality talent across Africa efficiently"
- "Help me build professional credibility in my field"
- "Help me access opportunities across 54 African countries"
- "Help me grow my business through the right professional connections"

Differentiation: Africa-first rather than Africa-adapted, deep integration of formal employment + freelance + internship + procurement in one network.

## Feature Evaluation Framework

For any proposed feature, answer:
1. Which specific user job does this serve?
2. What metric will prove it's working?
3. What is the smallest version we can ship in < 1 sprint?
4. What assumption are we testing?
5. What do we do if the metric doesn't move?
